Mission

Building Charleston into a recognized tech hub

CharlestonHacks is a federally recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to growing Charleston's technology community through high-quality events, open collaboration, and inclusive membership. We believe a thriving tech ecosystem begins with community — and membership is always free.

About

What we believe

These principles guide how we build community and support each other.

People Matter Most

We prioritize trust, inclusion, and human relationships over hype, metrics, or ego.

We Are Independent

We stay community-led so decisions serve members first and keep our mission clear.

We Are Connectors

We bring builders together across disciplines to spark collaborations that would not happen alone.

We Are Experimental

We test ideas in public, learn quickly, and treat iteration as a core part of progress.

We Help Others

We share knowledge, mentorship, and resources so more people can build with confidence.

We Think Big Picture

We focus on long-term impact for Charleston's tech ecosystem, not just short-term wins.
