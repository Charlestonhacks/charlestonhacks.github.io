FAQ

Before you register

Who can participate?

Builders interested in software, hardware, robotics, AI systems, developer tools, creative technology, or research prototypes are welcome.

Do I need a team before arriving?

No. You may register individually or with a team.

How large can teams be?

Teams may have 1–4 people. The AI agent does not count toward that limit.

Must every team use an AI agent?

Yes. Every team works with a persistent AI agent. People still set the direction, review the work, and make final decisions.

Which AI tools may teams use?

Teams may use Claude, Codex, Gemini, GPT-based tools, open-source agents, and other specialist tools alongside their AI teammate.

What should participants bring?

Bring a laptop, charger, headphones, your development tools, Git access, and any hardware your project needs.

What must teams submit?

Submit a functional prototype on Devpost by 5:00 PM EDT on Sunday, October 25. Include a project description, source-repository access whenever possible, your AI collaboration story, and be ready to present live.

Is a prerecorded video required?

No prerecorded project video is required.

How do live presentations work?

Teams present their project live. Plan for a 5-minute presentation followed by 3 minutes of judge Q&A, beginning at 6:00 PM EDT on Sunday.
